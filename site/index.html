    Methods & evaluation

    Every model here is scored against simple baselines on data it never saw. If it didn't beat them, it wouldn't be here.

    Data

    • RTB Average Monthly Rent Report (CSO table RIQ02, via the PxStat API): average rent for newly registered tenancies by location, bedrooms and property type, quarterly since 2007 Q4. Licensed CC BY 4.0.
    • Property Price Register (Property Services Regulatory Authority): every residential sale since 2010. Non-market sales are excluded and VAT-exclusive new-build prices are grossed up by 13.5%. Sales are placed in a district from the Eircode routing key, or the address when there's no Eircode.

    Limitations

    • RTB figures are averages for new tenancies in a segment, not listing prices; a specific flat can sit well outside the range for good reasons (condition, furnishing, exact location).
    • Postal-district averages after 2021 are reconstructed, not official; their validated error is shown above.
    • Gross yield ignores costs, vacancy and tax, and compares all-property averages; it's a market indicator, not an investment return.
